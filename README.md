# Happiness in Leadership: Managing Happiness

An interactive summary of what I learned in **Managing Happiness**, the course by Arthur C. Brooks (HarvardX), funded by the Learning Budget of [Bob W.](https://bobw.co/)

- **Live page:** https://marvinbonazzo.github.io/happiness-in-leadership/
- **Italian version:** https://marvinbonazzo.github.io/happiness-in-leadership/#it
- **PDF:** [Happiness-in-Leadership.pdf](Happiness-in-Leadership.pdf)

## What's inside

- Six modules plus a closing one, *What I bring to the team*
- Diagrams and practical examples
- Interactive sliders
- English and Italian

## How it's made

- A single HTML file, no dependencies
- The Figtree font, loaded from Google Fonts
- No data is stored, except the chosen language in your browser

## Note

Texts and summaries are my own, based on what I learned in the course. Charts show indicative trends, not exact data. Not affiliated with Harvard or HarvardX.
